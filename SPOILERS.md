# Restored dialogue

Some of the fixed dialogues can be quite far in the walkthrough and not everywhere have a save at hand to reach it but still might wanna know what was exactly recovered. 

So per suggestion by [robinsontfp](https://bsky.app/profile/did:plc:vntuz4iwhgo6jmqm2zzphcri), I'm including content of dialogues that previously were inaccessible but made working by this patch.

Check the same sections in [changelog](./CHANGELOG.md) for details on these dialogues.

*Note*:
- Voiced lines are marked as `(voiced)`, that's in vanilla game files, just inaccessible to bugs
- `(my writing` denotes player options that I had to create myself to provide access to existing but inaccessible NPC responses. I tried to keep them as simple as possible and usually just repeat similar player lines existing for other topics.
- Dumb player options are omitted unless they lead to a different NPC response

# Version 1.1.0

## Murgo dialogue on Torin Quarry

>**Player**: What do you know about this place?  
>**Murgo**: This is place we get big rock!  Now we get it and go home!

## Torian dialogue on Hardin's Pass

>**Player**: What do you know of this place?  
>**Torian(voiced)**: I think we marched through here on our way to battle the Molochean Hand.  I don't remember the name of it...

## Virgil and Z'an Al'urin dialogue on Ring of Brodgar

>**Player**: What do you know about this place?  
>**Virgil(voiced)**: This is the Ring of Brodgar. Joachim told me that it was what the Panarii originally based their religion around, but it seems to have become unimportant, somehow. He was rather upset about it, as a matter of fact. Joachim's a bit of a purist, he is. I wish I could remember what its importance was...

>**Player**: What do you know about this place? [Ring of Brodgar.]  
>**Z'an Al'urin(voiced)**: [She closes her eyes.]  I see a great wound which strains against its dressing. I see a father's sorrow.   And I see an old truth waiting to be found.  [She opens her eyes.]  This is a powerful place, full of pain and magick.  There is much to be learned, here...

## Magnus dialogue on Isle of Despair

>**Player**: What can you tell me about the Isle of Despair, Magnus?  
>**Magnus(voiced)**: What?  An island?  No self-respecting dwarf makes it his business to know about islands!  Too much bloody water around it!  Give a dwarf mountains and valleys, but keep him away from water!  Uggh!  The thought...!

## Magnus dialogue about the Wheel Clan
